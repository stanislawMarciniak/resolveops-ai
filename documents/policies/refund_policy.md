# Refund Policy

## Refund eligibility

Refunds may be issued only for confirmed duplicate payments, incorrect charges, or other explicitly approved billing adjustments.

An unmatched payment is not by itself evidence that a refund is required.

## Approval

Refund operations require explicit human approval.

A refund must never be used as a substitute for correcting a payment-to-invoice matching error when the payment legitimately belongs to an outstanding invoice.
