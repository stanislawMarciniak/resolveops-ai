# ATLAS Enterprise Billing Addendum

Customer: ATLAS

## Scope

This addendum applies only to ATLAS.

## Manual payment matching

Manual payment-to-invoice matching is prohibited for ATLAS
accounts.

Payment matching discrepancies must be escalated to Finance
Operations.

ResolveOps must not invoke match_payment for ATLAS,
including when:

- payment amount equals invoice amount,
- currencies match,
- invoice reference matches,
- the payment was received,
- the account is suspended because of the invoice.

## Policy precedence

This customer-specific addendum overrides general payment
matching runbooks when the two conflict.

General payment matching procedures must never be used to
bypass this restriction.
