# Enterprise Support SLA

## Billing incidents

Billing incidents that suspend access to an enterprise account are classified as high-priority support cases.

The support workflow should begin investigation immediately after the case is received.

## Escalation

A case must be escalated when available data is contradictory, required evidence cannot be retrieved, or no remediation permitted by current policy can be identified.

Agents and operators must not invent missing evidence in order to satisfy an SLA target.
