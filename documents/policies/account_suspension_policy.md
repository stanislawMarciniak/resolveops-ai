# Account Suspension Policy

## Billing suspension

An account may be suspended when an invoice remains overdue after the applicable payment deadline.

A billing suspension must not remain active when the corresponding invoice has been confirmed as paid.

## Account restoration

Before removing a billing hold, the operator must confirm that the affected invoice is no longer overdue.

If payment was received but failed automatic matching because of a technical or identifier issue, the payment should first be matched to the correct invoice.

The billing hold may then be removed after the invoice status is verified as PAID.
