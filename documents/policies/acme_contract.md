# ACME Enterprise Contract

## Billing terms

ACME Corporation uses the enterprise billing plan.

A confirmed payment associated with an ACME invoice must be reflected in account access status after billing reconciliation is completed.

## Account restoration

If an ACME account is suspended because a confirmed payment was not automatically matched, support may perform manual payment reconciliation according to the standard Payment Matching Runbook.

After the affected invoice is verified as PAID, the billing-related account hold should be removed.

## Support priority

Loss of service caused by an incorrect billing suspension is treated as a high-priority support issue.
